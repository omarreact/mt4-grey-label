BEFORE YOU BUILD

The questions that matter.

Clear requirements make a more coherent brokerage technology stack. Here is what to confirm before you start.

Discuss your requirements ↗
01Can we plan both MT4 and MT5 platforms?+

Yes. We can assess both platform paths, including provider arrangements, technical prerequisites and the operating model you need. Actual access depends on participating licence holders.

02Can our existing CRM connect to the platform?+

Integration is assessed against available APIs, permissions and the CRM provider's capabilities. Each client lifecycle and trading-account workflow should be validated before launch.

03Is the illustrated dashboard a live platform?+

No. The workspace shown here is a design mockup explaining the proposed relationships between platform, CRM and execution systems—not a connected trading environment.

04When is a configuration ready to launch?+

After the chosen provider arrangements, integrations, security controls, user journeys, operational documentation and support responsibilities have been tested and approved.
