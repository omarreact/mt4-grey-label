Frequently Asked Questions

01What is a forex grey label?+

It's an arrangement where your brokerage operates under its own brand on a MetaTrader server run by a licence holder. You manage your clients and trading conditions through manager access.

02Can I offer both MT4 and MT5?+

Yes. Many brokers run both so traders can choose the platform they prefer.

03Can I get my own MT4 licence?+

New MT4 licences are no longer issued by the platform vendor, so a grey label through an existing licence holder is the practical route.

04What do PAMM and MAM plugins do?+

They let a money manager trade on behalf of multiple investors, with results, allocations and fees handled automatically.

05Do you provide liquidity?+

We set up bridge technology and connect you to liquidity providers. Pricing and terms come from your agreements with those providers.

06Does the CRM work with both platforms?+

Yes. It connects to MT4 and MT5 through the manager API, so clients can hold accounts on either.

07Are you a broker or a regulated company?+

No. We're a technology provider. We don't hold client funds, offer trading services or give investment advice.

08Do I need a licence to run a brokerage?+

It depends on your business model and where your clients are. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction.

09How long does it take to launch?+

Platform setup is usually quicker than the business side. Company formation, payment provider approval and licensing tend to decide the timeline.

10Can I move to white label later?+

Many brokers plan to. Whether it's possible depends on licence holder availability and vendor terms at the time.
