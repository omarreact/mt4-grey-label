Frequently asked

Everything you need to know.

Key distinctions and practical considerations before choosing the technology behind your brokerage.

01What is a forex grey label?+

A grey-label arrangement lets a brokerage offer a branded trading experience while the core platform server is operated by an existing licence holder. Scope, administrative access and contractual rights depend on the provider.

02Can my brokerage offer MT4 and MT5?+

Potentially yes, provided your platform partners support both. Availability, commercial terms and jurisdictional restrictions should be confirmed before any commitment.

03How do CRM and liquidity connect to the platform?+

Typically, a broker CRM uses platform management integrations for client and account operations, while bridge software manages connections to liquidity providers. The architecture varies by vendor and execution model.

04Does your technology replace licensing or compliance?+

No. Technology infrastructure does not replace legal authorization, applicable licensing, compliance procedures or independent professional advice. Those obligations depend on where and how the brokerage operates.

05Can we expand the platform setup later?+

Many brokerages add features, integrations and capacity as they grow. The path to a larger arrangement or different licence structure depends on the platform provider, contracts and technical requirements.
