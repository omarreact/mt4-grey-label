Some basics to consider before planning your brokerage technology.

What is a MetaTrader grey label?+

A grey label generally uses the platform infrastructure of a participating licence holder, with a level of branding and operating control determined by the commercial arrangement.

Can MT4 and MT5 be used together?+

Potentially. The available options depend on the relevant licence-holder arrangements, technical integrations and the business needs you define.

Can a CRM integrate with trading accounts?+

Yes, subject to supported platform interfaces and your CRM provider. Integration scope should be validated against actual access and workflows.

Do you provide brokerage or investment services?+

No. This page describes technology and integration services, not client brokerage services, investment advice or a guarantee of regulatory approval.
