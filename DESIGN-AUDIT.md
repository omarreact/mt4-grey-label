## Scope and handling

- All marketing copy comes from the supplied Homepage Google Doc only; where the template described pricing, endorsements, or irrelevant financial tools, the corresponding MT4 Grey Label sections were substituted.
- Each HTML page includes a full accessible page, navigation, distinct hero, service presentation, setup process, technology stack, audiences, comparisons, FAQ and CTA.
- The archive's vendor CSS, JS, paid template code, copyrighted images, brand logos and tracking scripts are not incorporated into the new standalone pages.
- Distinct section composition was reviewed via generated desktop/mobile screenshots and checked at widths 320, 390, 768, 1440px.
