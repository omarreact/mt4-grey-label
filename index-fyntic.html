More Systems Than Most Founders Expect

Ask ten traders which platform they use and you'll hear MetaTrader again and again. Some swear by MT4 and the expert advisors they've built up over years. Others have moved to MT5 for its wider markets and newer tools. A brokerage that can offer either, under its own name, starts with an advantage.

A forex grey label makes that possible without holding a platform licence yourself. Your brokerage runs on an established MetaTrader server, while you manage your own traders, groups and conditions. We set that up and connect the systems a working brokerage depends on, from the CRM and plugins to liquidity, website and hosting.

The platform is the part everyone thinks about first. Then the list grows.

You need a CRM to sign clients up, verify them and track their money. Plugins if you want to offer managed accounts. A liquidity connection if trades are going to the market. A website that turns visitors into registrations. Hosting to keep it all running.

There's another wrinkle that catches people out. Whether you can offer MT4 or MT5 at all depends on who holds the licence. The platform vendor stopped issuing new MT4 licences years ago and has been selective about newer arrangements too. In practice, your options depend on the licence holders you work with and their current terms.

That's a lot to coordinate. It's also exactly what we do.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a forex grey label?

It's an arrangement where your brokerage operates under its own brand on a MetaTrader server run by a licence holder. You manage your clients and trading conditions through manager access.

Can I offer both MT4 and MT5?

Yes. Many brokers run both so traders can choose the platform they prefer.

Can I get my own MT4 licence?

New MT4 licences are no longer issued by the platform vendor, so a grey label through an existing licence holder is the practical route.

What do PAMM and MAM plugins do?

They let a money manager trade on behalf of multiple investors, with results, allocations and fees handled automatically.

Do you provide liquidity?

We set up bridge technology and connect you to liquidity providers. Pricing and terms come from your agreements with those providers.

Does the CRM work with both platforms?

Yes. It connects to MT4 and MT5 through the manager API, so clients can hold accounts on either.

Are you a broker or a regulated company?

No. We're a technology provider. We don't hold client funds, offer trading services or give investment advice.

Do I need a licence to run a brokerage?

It depends on your business model and where your clients are. Regulatory requirements vary by jurisdiction.

How long does it take to launch?

Platform setup is usually quicker than the business side. Company formation, payment provider approval and licensing tend to decide the timeline.

Can I move to white label later?

Many brokers plan to. Whether it's possible depends on licence holder availability and vendor terms at the time.
