03 / QUICK CLARITY

Got questions?
We've got context.

Get the basics clear before designing a full brokerage infrastructure plan.

Is the grey label the same as a platform licence?

No. Grey label arrangements typically use licensed infrastructure operated by another provider. Access, permissions and availability depend on the agreement.

What does the CRM actually do?

A broker CRM supports account opening, verification workflows, customer operations and partner management. It complements rather than replaces the trading platform.

Can I launch with only the modules I need?

Yes, subject to integration requirements. We can discuss a tailored technology scope based on your brokerage model.

Are brokerage licensing and compliance included?

Technology services are distinct from regulatory permissions. Obtain independent jurisdiction-specific legal and compliance advice.
