03 / CLARITY FIRST

Questions, without the noise.

Understand the building blocks before selecting a brokerage technology setup.

What exactly is a grey label?

A grey label is an arrangement where a brokerage offers a branded trading experience using infrastructure operated by an existing platform licence holder. Terms and availability depend on the provider.

Can MT4 and MT5 run alongside each other?

Potentially, yes. A dual-platform setup depends on the available providers, commercial arrangements and integration scope.

Does a CRM replace the trading platform?

No. The CRM manages onboarding and back-office workflows, while the trading platform supports trader-facing activity. Integrations connect the two.

Is licensing and compliance included?

Technology services do not replace legal or regulatory authorization. Licensing requirements depend on where and how the brokerage operates.
