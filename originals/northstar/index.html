03 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good decisions start with clarity.

Practical answers about what is included in a brokerage technology stack.

What makes grey label different from white label?

A grey label typically uses existing licensed infrastructure with a branded client experience. The range of control and responsibilities differs by provider and agreement.

Can we choose only some of the six solutions?

Yes. The final scope can be structured around your operating requirements, provided dependencies and compatibility are assessed.

How do platforms connect to a CRM?

Typically through supported APIs or manager interfaces, depending on platform access and CRM capabilities.

Do technology services include regulatory approval?

No. Authorization, compliance and licensing requirements depend on your jurisdiction and business model.
