Straight answers

Your questions.
Considered responses.

What is a forex grey label?

An arrangement in which your brokerage operates under its own brand on a MetaTrader server run by a licence holder. You manage clients and trading conditions through manager access.

Can I offer both MT4 and MT5?

Yes, subject to the available arrangements with licence holders. Many brokers offer both to give traders a choice.

Can I get my own MT4 licence?

New MT4 licences are no longer issued by the platform vendor, so using an existing licence holder is the practical route.

What do PAMM and MAM plugins do?

They let a money manager trade on behalf of multiple investors, with allocations and fees handled through supporting technology.

Do you provide liquidity?

We configure bridge technology and connections to liquidity providers. Pricing and commercial terms are governed by your agreements with those providers.

Does the CRM work with both platforms?

The CRM connects to MT4 and MT5 through their manager APIs so clients can hold accounts on either.

Are you a broker or a regulated company?

We are a technology provider, not a brokerage. We do not hold client funds, offer trading services or give investment advice.

Do I need a licence to run a brokerage?

Requirements depend on your business model and the jurisdictions in which you operate or serve clients. Obtain professional regulatory advice.

How long does it take to launch?

The business side often determines the timeline: formation, payment approval and licensing can take longer than technical setup.

Can I move to white label later?

That may be possible as your brokerage grows, depending on licence-holder availability and vendor terms at the time.
