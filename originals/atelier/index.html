04 / Questions worth asking

The finer points.

Clear explanations for foundational brokerage technology decisions.

What does a grey label provide?

A branded trading experience using existing licensed platform infrastructure. Specific access, control and responsibilities vary by licence holder.

What is the role of the CRM?

It coordinates back-office workflows such as onboarding, verification and account management, and can connect with trading platform infrastructure.

Can I add managed accounts later?

Potentially. PAMM/MAM integration depends on the platform provider, compatible modules and your service model.

Do I still need regulatory advice?

Yes. Technology configuration is separate from the legal and regulatory obligations of operating a brokerage.
